StreamClipper · last updated 17 August 2026

1. What this is

StreamClipper is a personal, non-commercial tool that cuts highlights from the operator's own game stream recordings and publishes them to the operator's own social accounts. It runs on the operator's own computer.

2. Who may use it

Only the operator — the owner of the connected accounts. The tool is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else. There is no registration, and access is restricted to a single pre-approved Telegram account.

3. Content

All processed content is created by the operator: recordings of their own streams. The operator is responsible for that content complying with the rules of any platform it is published to.

4. Publishing

Nothing is published automatically. Each upload happens only after the operator reviews the clip and presses a button. The operator can revoke the tool's access to any connected account at any time, from that platform's own settings or with the tool's disconnect command.

5. Third-party platforms

Uploads go through the official APIs of YouTube and TikTok. Use of those platforms is governed by their own terms; this tool does not modify or override them, and does not attempt to bypass any platform limit or restriction.

6. No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may produce imperfect clips, fail to upload, or stop working when a platform changes its API. The operator uses it at their own risk.

7. Changes

These terms may be updated as the tool changes. The current version is always the one on this page.
