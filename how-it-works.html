How it works

The path a recording takes, from the moment the stream ends to a published clip.

  1. The recording arrives

    The streaming software writes the recording into a folder on the operator's own machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere at this point.

  2. The moments are found

    Audio is analysed for reactions, speech around them is transcribed locally, and the moments worth clipping are selected. A three-hour recording usually yields a handful.

  3. The clips are built

    Each moment is rendered as a vertical clip: webcam above, gameplay below, captions at the seam, sound normalised.

  4. The clips are checked

    Automatic verification rejects anything broken — silence, black frames, a missing camera, a sentence cut in half.

  5. The operator reviews them

    Clips arrive in a private Telegram chat with their suggested title, description and tags. They can be re-cut or rewritten from there.

  6. Publishing happens on approval

    Each platform has its own button. Pressing it uploads that one clip. Nothing is scheduled, batched or published automatically.

What leaves the machine

DataWhere it goes
The stream recordingNowhere. It stays on the operator's disk.
Transcript excerptsTo Anthropic's Claude API, to choose moments and write captions.
Finished clipsTo Telegram for review, and to a platform only when the operator presses its button.

Access to a platform can be revoked at any time, either from that platform's own settings or with the tool's disconnect command.
