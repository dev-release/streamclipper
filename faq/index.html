FAQ

The questions that come up most often.

Is StreamClipper a service I can sign up for?
No. It is a personal tool that runs on its operator's own computer. There is no registration, no hosted service and no other users.
Whose content does it process?
Only the operator's own stream recordings, uploaded to the operator's own accounts.
Does anything get published automatically?
No. Every clip is reviewed first, and every upload starts with the operator pressing a button for that specific clip.
What does it read from the platforms?
From TikTok: the account name and the privacy options available to that account, both needed for the confirmation screen shown before publishing. From YouTube: the view count of videos it uploaded itself. Nothing else — no followers, no comments, no other people's content.
Where are the recordings stored?
On the operator's own disk. They are deleted by the operator when the space is needed. They are never uploaded anywhere.
How is access to an account granted?
Through the platform's own authorisation screen. The operator sees which permissions are being requested and approves them. Access can be revoked from the platform's settings at any time.
What happens to a clip that fails a check?
It is not delivered. The operator is told what was wrong with it, and can re-cut it.
Can captions or the camera overlay be turned off?
Yes, both. They are on by default because that is the standard shape of short-form video.
