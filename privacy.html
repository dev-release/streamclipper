Privacy Policy

StreamClipper · last updated 17 August 2026

StreamClipper is a personal tool operated by a single individual, the owner of the connected accounts. It is not a service offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool handles

  • Stream recordings — video files created by the operator, stored only on the operator's own computer.
  • Transcripts — speech from those recordings, produced locally and stored alongside them.
  • Access tokens — credentials the operator grants for their own YouTube and TikTok accounts, stored in files readable only by the operator's system account.

What is not collected

  • No data about any other person or account
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or cookies
  • No accounts, sign-ups or user profiles
  • Nothing is read from TikTok — the integration only uploads

Where data goes

Video files never leave the operator's computer, except for finished clips that the operator explicitly chooses to publish. Excerpts of the transcript are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to select moments and write captions. Finished clips are sent to Telegram for the operator's own review, and to YouTube or TikTok only when the operator presses the corresponding button.

Retention

Recordings, clips and transcripts stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them. Access tokens remain until the operator disconnects the service, which deletes them from disk.

Third-party services

  • Google / YouTube Data API — to upload videos to the operator's channel
  • TikTok Content Posting API — to upload videos to the operator's account
  • Telegram Bot API — to deliver clips to the operator for review
  • Anthropic Claude API — to select moments and write captions from transcript text

Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Since no third-party personal data is collected, there is nothing to request, export or erase on behalf of another person. The operator can revoke the tool's access at any time from their Google or TikTok account settings.
